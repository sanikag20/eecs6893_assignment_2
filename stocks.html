Stock Price Tracker

This page uses split-adjusted, end-of-day closing prices. Each request searches through yesterday in New York and compares the two most recent returned trading dates.

Change = latest daily close − previous trading close. Green means an increase, red means a decrease, and gray means no change. The signs and direction labels provide the same information in text.

Each completed request is followed by a 90-second pause before the next request. New rows are appended to the history. Daily prices may repeat across refreshes.
