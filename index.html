The ten-bin and twenty-bin histograms use identical observations and horizontal limits, but the twenty-bin version splits each wider interval in two and reveals finer local variation. The interactive histogram makes it possible to inspect how precipitation, maximum temperature, minimum temperature, and wind differ in shape and range.
